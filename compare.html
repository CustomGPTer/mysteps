What MySteps does not do

MySteps has no workouts or training plans, and it does not read steps from a watch. It is not on Google Play, so you install it from this site and it tells you when a new version is out. It is not medical advice.

Where the others do more

Leap Fitness Group's Step Counter includes guided workouts and backs up to Google Drive. StepsApp links with Google Fit. Both are on Google Play and update themselves.
