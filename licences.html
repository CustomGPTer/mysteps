The MySteps food list

The food list inside MySteps is a trimmed version of the Open Food Facts database (UK products with full, consistent nutrition and a barcode), combined with the UK government's composition of foods tables. As the Open Database Licence asks, this trimmed list is available to anyone, under the same licence.
