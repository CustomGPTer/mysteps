Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026. This policy covers the MySteps app for Android (package uk.jj.mysteps) and this website. MySteps is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom.

The short version

  • Your steps, walks, routes, meals and health records are kept on your phone. They're never sent to the developer or to anyone else.
  • MySteps goes online for one thing only: to ask GitHub whether a newer version is out. You can switch that off.
  • There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking, in the app or on this site.

What stays on your phone

Everything you record: your steps by the hour, active minutes and floors, walks and any GPS routes, water, weight, blood pressure, heart rate, sleep, your food diary, your own foods and meals, journeys, badges and settings. They're stored in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read.

The details you give about yourself (sex, age, height and weight) are used only to work out distance, calories and your food target.

What the app can reach

  • Physical activity, to read the step sensor.
  • Location, only during a walk you start, and only if you switch on route recording. The route is kept with that walk on your phone.
  • Camera, only while you scan a barcode or measure your heart rate. Pictures are looked at on the phone and thrown away straight away. Nothing is recorded or saved apart from the barcode or the heart rate.
  • Notifications, for the step-counting notice and the reminders you choose.
  • Files and folders you pick, to save a backup or a report, read a backup you choose, and save the nightly backup.
  • Health Connect, only if you switch it on (see below).

MySteps has no access to your contacts, photos, messages, microphone or files you haven't picked.

The update check

Once a day, if you leave the setting on, MySteps asks GitHub's public releases address for MySteps whether there's a newer version. Like any web request, this reveals your IP address to GitHub, which handles it under GitHub's privacy statement. Nothing about you or your records is sent. You can switch it off in You, under Updates.

The food list and barcode reading are built into the app, so nothing you eat is ever looked up online.

Health information

Your health records are special category data under UK data protection law. Because they never leave your phone (unless you save a backup, share a report or switch on Health Connect sharing), the developer never holds or processes them.

Health Connect

Health Connect is part of Android and keeps health data on your phone. MySteps uses it only if you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time in MySteps or in Health Connect.

  • Steps in: if you allow it, MySteps reads step counts other apps have recorded, such as your watch's app. For each hour it counts whichever is larger, its own steps or the other app's.
  • Records out: if you allow it, MySteps writes your weight, blood pressure, heart rate, sleep and water records to Health Connect, so your other health apps can use them.

MySteps doesn't use Health Connect data for advertising, doesn't sell it, and doesn't send it anywhere.

Backups and reports

A backup file holds all your records and settings. It goes only where you save it. If you choose a folder for nightly backups, and that folder is kept in sync by a cloud storage app, that app's own privacy terms apply to the copies there. A monthly report is made on your phone and goes only where you share or save it.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your records are kept until you delete them. You can delete single entries in the app. Uninstalling MySteps deletes everything it stored on the phone, though not any backup files or reports you saved elsewhere, or records you shared with Health Connect.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights over personal data held about you. The developer holds none, so there's nothing to request, but you're welcome to ask. You can also contact the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and has no analytics or tracking. GitHub may keep standard server logs, as described in its privacy statement.

Children

MySteps isn't aimed at children under 13. It collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date, and noted in the release notes.

Contact

For a question about privacy, please open an issue on GitHub.
