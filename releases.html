Version 1.0.1

Tidier Health page

LatestReleased 21 November 2026

Fixed

  • On the Health page, “Not logged” no longer runs off the edge or overlaps the text below it, and the four cards are now the same size in each row.
  • Text in the switch buttons and the small cards on Today now fits on phones with larger text, rather than being cut short.

Version 1.0.0

First release

Released 21 November 2026

Added

  • Step counting in the background with your phone's step sensor, or from motion on phones without one, carrying on after a restart.
  • Today: a goal ring, distance, walking calories, active minutes, floors climbed, an hour-by-hour chart and the week.
  • Timed walks with pause and resume, and an optional GPS route drawn as an outline, with no map service.
  • Trends for steps, distance, calories, active minutes and floors by day, week, month and year, with records.
  • Water with your own cup sizes, weight with a trend line and safe goals, blood pressure with NHS, European or American ranges, heart rate from the camera or typed in, and a sleep log with step insights.
  • A food diary with 147,689 foods built in: 2,853 everyday UK foods and 144,836 UK branded products. Search as you type, with spelling help and British and American names.
  • Barcode scanning on the phone, your own foods and saved meals and recipes, and a daily calorie target that never goes below a safe level.
  • Fair streaks, with one rest day a week, and 82 badges in nine groups from bronze to platinum, with a celebration for each.
  • Fifteen journeys, eight around the UK and seven around the world, with real stops and a drawing of the route.
  • Nine kinds of reminder with quiet hours, and notifications when you reach your goal or earn a badge.
  • Eight home-screen widgets, including water with a one-tap add button.
  • A monthly report as a two-page PDF, to share or save.
  • Backup to a file, restore with Undo, and a nightly backup to a folder you choose, keeping the newest seven.
  • Health Connect: steps from a watch, counted fairly hour by hour, and sharing your records with other health apps.
  • Light, dark and automatic themes, and miles or kilometres, stones, pounds or kilograms.
  • A banner on Today when a newer version is ready.
